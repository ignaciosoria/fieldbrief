FOLUP — PROSPECTING BRIEF
Prepared 7 October 2026. Product descriptions checked against the repository.
Targeting below is an initial hypothesis to validate, not a proven ICP or market result.

POSITIONING
Folup helps field salespeople turn their own post-visit voice recap into clear
follow-ups, a CRM-ready note, and a commercially useful Smart Next Step.
Lead with the moment after a customer visit, not with AI models or transcription.
Suggested positioning: “Leave the visit. Say what happened. Know what to do next.”

WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES
1. The rep records their own recap after a visit, in natural English or Spanish.
   This is not a meeting bot joining calls or secretly recording customers.
2. Speech is transcribed and interpreted into people, companies, key details,
   commitments, relevant timing and commercial context.
3. Explicit actions are extracted: send the spec sheet, provide pricing, call a
   contact, check a trial, or follow up on an order.
4. When useful, Smart Next Step proposes an additional action based on the note:
   for example, check what technical approval is needed before a product trial.
   Recommendations are not commitments made by the customer. They can be wrong.
5. The rep reviews and can correct the output by voice.
6. After connecting Google Calendar, the rep can add chosen actions directly,
   with a short title, description, date and time. No contact invitations are sent.
7. Copy to CRM produces a readable visit note for pasting into the existing CRM.
   This is not automatic two-way CRM sync or automatic Salesforce data entry.
8. Saved notes are available in history. Context explicitly requested for research
   can appear in the CRM output; do not make this the campaign's main promise.

SMART NEXT STEP EXAMPLE — ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT A CUSTOMER TESTIMONIAL
Input: “Maya likes the product, but her engineer has to review it before a trial.
Send her the spec sheet tomorrow, no prices. She will speak to him Thursday.”
Explicit action: Send Maya the spec sheet tomorrow without pricing.
Potential recommendation: Check with Maya after the technical discussion to
understand what her engineer still needs before approving a trial.
Not: assume the trial is approved, invent access to the engineer, or schedule a
meeting that nobody agreed to.

OFFER
Current code describes a 14-day free trial, no card, starting at the first
processed note, with up to 100 notes. Smart Next Step is included in the trial.
The displayed Pro price is $19/month. Verify the deployed checkout before using
the price in a campaign. Never promise unlimited inference or guaranteed accuracy.

FIRST ICP TO TEST
English-speaking US field sales reps in agricultural inputs and distribution:
crop nutrition, fertilizers, biostimulants and related agricultural supplies.
Prioritize people who visit growers, dealers or farms, discuss product trials,
manage technical approval, quote products and personally own the next follow-up.
Start with California and Florida as separate cohorts if enough qualified leads
exist. Geography and company size are starting hypotheses, not requirements.

APOLLO TITLE IDEAS
Territory Sales Representative; Territory Manager; Outside Sales Representative;
Field Sales Representative; Technical Sales Representative; Regional Sales
Representative; Agricultural Sales Representative; independent sales agent.
Do not assume a Territory Manager is a field seller from the title alone.

ACCOUNT FILTER IDEAS
Agricultural inputs, crop nutrition, fertilizer, biostimulants, agricultural
distribution. Begin with roughly 10–200 employees as a discovery range, not a
hard exclusion. Confirm each company's actual business from public information.
Relevant words: grower relationships, farm visits, territory, field trials,
dealer network, crop advisor, technical sales. Avoid keyword-only qualification.

QUALIFICATION SIGNALS
- Regular in-person customer visits and responsibility for follow-up.
- Product sales involving trials, samples, quotes or technical stakeholders.
- CRM or manual note administration that happens after visits.
- A Google account and willingness to use Google Calendar.
- Individual ability to trial a lightweight tool without a long procurement cycle.
Public evidence of field visits is a stronger signal than a generic “sales” title.

LOWER-FIT LEADS FOR THE FIRST CAMPAIGN
Desk-only SDRs, recruiters, generic digital agencies, ecommerce sellers, advisors
with no commercial sales responsibility, and people seeking a full CRM replacement.
Independent advisors can fit if they sell products or own commercial follow-ups;
an agronomist title alone is not sufficient. Outlook-only teams have more friction
because the current direct calendar integration is Google Calendar.
Avoid enterprises requiring SSO, SOC 2, team administration or deep CRM sync unless
those capabilities have been separately verified. Do not claim them.

HOW TO PERSONALIZE
Use one verified professional fact about their territory, products or field role.
Connect it to remembering promises after visits. Do not pretend to know that a
specific person loses sales, forgets follow-ups, or has bad CRM discipline.
Do not invent shared contacts, familiarity, customer logos, testimonials, revenue
uplift, time saved, or accuracy percentages.

CAMPAIGN ANGLE
“After a grower visit, do you type up the notes later? Folup turns a quick voice
recap into follow-ups, a CRM note, and a suggested next move for the sale.”
Keep one call to action. Do not lead with every feature, research, model names,
Decisions API, or a claim that Folup closes deals automatically.

DESTINATION
Recommended first-touch destination after landing deployment: https://www.folup.app/
Use /try when explicitly inviting the prospect to explore a sample visit.
The sample walkthrough needs no account; recording their own note needs sign-in.
The sample is prewritten, not a live AI evaluation of the visitor's input.
Suggested campaign URL after attribution is implemented:
https://www.folup.app/?utm_source=apollo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=ag_field_pilot
Current analytics strips UTMs: this URL does NOT currently produce campaign
attribution in PostHog. Do not put a prospect's email or name in a URL.

MEASUREMENT BEFORE SCALING
Start with one coherent cohort of about 50 qualified contacts, not many tiny
variants. Review responses and whether visitors create their first useful note.
Current PostHog only receives anonymous pageviews; it cannot show named prospects
or the full signup-to-note-to-calendar funnel. Add minimal, privacy-safe campaign
and conversion measurement before treating traffic as campaign performance.
Desired events: demo opened, signup completed, first note processed, CRM copied,
calendar event successfully created, subscription activated. These are a proposed
measurement plan, not events already implemented.
Never transmit voice audio, transcripts, CRM text, contact names or calendar
descriptions into product analytics.
